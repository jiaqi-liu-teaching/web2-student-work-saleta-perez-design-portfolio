El Dorado

El Dorado is a historical museum located in Tenjo, Colombia based on the Musica people which focuses on the legend of El Dorado which is rumored to have come from the Muisca people and their gold work. I worked on El Dorado in 2025 to bring to life the lost history this museum focuses on three significant time periods for the Muisca people.
